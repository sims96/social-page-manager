Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how FB Page Manager ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Facebook Page management application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use FB Page Manager, we may collect the following information:

  • Facebook Account Information: When you connect your Facebook account, we receive access to your Facebook Pages that you manage, including page names, IDs, and page access tokens.
  • Page Content: We access your page's posts, comments, photos, videos, and engagement metrics to display them within the app.
  • Usage Data: Basic information about how you interact with the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Display your Facebook Page content and analytics
  • Allow you to create, publish, and manage posts on your Facebook Page
  • Enable you to respond to comments and engage with your audience
  • Provide page insights and performance metrics

3. Data Storage

Your Facebook access tokens are stored securely and are used only to communicate with the Facebook Graph API on your behalf. We do not store your Facebook password. Session data is temporary and expires after 24 hours.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only used to provide the services of this application and to communicate with Facebook's API.

5. Facebook Data

This app uses the Facebook Graph API. By using this app, you also agree to Facebook's Terms of Service and Data Policy. We only request the minimum permissions necessary to provide our services:

  • pages_show_list - To see your Facebook Pages
  • pages_read_engagement - To read your page's content and metrics
  • pages_manage_posts - To create and manage posts
  • pages_manage_engagement - To respond to comments

6. Data Deletion

You can disconnect your Facebook Page from this app at any time using the "Disconnect" button. This will remove your access token from our system. You can also revoke access through your Facebook Settings under "Business Integrations".

7. Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All communication with Facebook's API is done over secure HTTPS connections.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the application or visit our Facebook Page.
